The Atelier Legacy

Preserving the Grandeur of Handwoven Heritage

Arbachin (আরবাচীন) was founded with a singular conviction: to resurrect the regal splendor of subcontinental textiles and bring timeless artisanal luxury into the contemporary wardrobe.

From the historic loom clusters of Banaras to the ancestral master embroiderers of Bengal, every yard of silk is touched by generational dedication. Our pieces are not merely garments—they are heirloom treasures crafted to be cherished across generations.
